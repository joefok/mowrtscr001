Diagnostics and limitations 

  
    
    
  • A TURN allocation test does not prove that media relay ports are reachable.
    • 
    
  • If no relay candidate is obtained, inspect coturn, its listening port,
        credentials, and firewalls.
    • 
    
  • Screen capture requires HTTPS and browser permission.
    • 
    
  • One controlling teacher per room; multiple rooms are supported.
    • 
    
  • Keep the teacher browser open. No coordinator failover is included.
    • 
    
  • Firebase anonymous authentication does not verify school identities.
    • 
    
  • TURN address and test credentials are public in this source.
    • 
    
  • Production requires stronger access control, temporary TURN credentials,
        abuse protection, and cleanup of old room records.
