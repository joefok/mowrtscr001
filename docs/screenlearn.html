Layout and classroom settings

Seat numbers run horizontally away from the selected corner, then continue on the next row. Changing the corner moves tiles but does not change their seat IDs, reservations, or shared links.

After you stop sharing, your seat remains temporarily reserved.

Students and assistance

Amber tiles indicate help requests. Open a tile to acknowledge or resolve help.

Network and diagnostics

Private staff credential:

   Room settings in a URL are applied only by an authorized controlling teacher.
   Student parameters cannot override classroom settings.
