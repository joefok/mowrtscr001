  2. Participant opens the invitation here; an answer downloads.
  3. Teacher accepts the answer file.

Tap a tile to enlarge. Maximum 64 positions. Students see seat metadata but do not receive other students' video. Grey means unavailable or uncertain, not proof of misconduct.

Diagnostics and limitations 

  

    HTTPS and a supported desktop browser are required for screen capture.
    Mobile browsers can view; screen-capture support is platform-dependent.
    No account authentication, automatic discovery, coordinator failover,
    or SFU is included. Additional teachers increase coordinator workload.
    Static screens may remain healthy when frames continue arriving.
    Test on your actual devices before classroom deployment.
