Classroom Screen Lobby

Teacher: Create room, enable Auto accept, share the room link.
Student: Open link, enter name, select a seat, and wait. No pairing answers or files.

Configure services, then create a room.

Lobby and connected students

Click an empty tile to join that seat.

Your browser always requires your approval to share a screen. Teacher acceptance cannot bypass this permission.

Diagnostics and implementation notes 

  
    
    
  • One controlling teacher per room. Multiple separate rooms are supported.
    • 
    
  • Firebase handles lobby discovery and automatic SDP signaling.
    • 
    
  • Video and WebRTC DataChannels are forced through coturn.
    • 
    
  • Keep the creating teacher's browser open. No automatic failover.
    • 
    
  • Screen sharing requires HTTPS and supported desktop-browser capture.
    • 
    
  • Grey means missing, disconnected, or possibly stalled video.
    • 
    
  • Static screens may remain healthy when encoded frames continue arriving.
    • 
    
  • Anonymous authentication does not verify real-world student identity.
    • 
    
  • Production needs school authentication, temporary TURN credentials,
      abuse protection, and cleanup of old Firebase room records.
    •
